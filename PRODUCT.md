# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js App Router, TypeScript strict, static export, React, scoped/global CSS. npm.

## Users and purpose
A flagship agency portfolio demonstration of a fictional architecture and interiors practice. Private clients, property owners, hospitality founders, cultural organizations and design collaborators should explore the work and assemble a thoughtful project brief. Visual quality and share presentation lead.

## Brand and truth
SECTION / OFFICE — Architecture · Interiors · Spatial Research. Spaces shaped by light, material, and everyday life. A self-initiated concept; all projects are fictional design studies and images are generated visualizations. No claims of real clients, built work, credentials, location, awards or outcomes.

## Catalog
01 Fold House — Residential; folded roof and protected courtyard.
02 Foundry Hall — Adaptive reuse; industrial shell and reversible exhibition insertions.
03 Common Ground — Cultural; neighborhood reading room and sheltered public threshold.
04 Quarry Rooms — Hospitality; stone walls and changing levels.
05 Frame Apartment — Interiors; joinery and shared light.

07 Northlight Studio — Workplace; diffuse daylight.
08 Passage Gallery — Commercial; thresholds and continuous display wall.

## Routes and journeys
Home → work (category/search, grid/list) → eight case studies → save/share → five-step brief → local review and download. Studio, process, three journal articles, contact, saved, my-brief, demo, demo/inbox, privacy and 404 complete the sitemap.

## Boundaries
No database, backend, API keys, authentication, actual email, cloud synchronization, payments, uploads or tracking. Browser data uses one versioned localStorage key with validated recovery. Public content and social metadata are rendered at build time. Deployment is not authorized; prepare a local static preview and exact HTTPS-origin deployment instructions.

## Acceptance
30 distinct coherent project images (five per flagship, three per remaining project), 3 conceptual SVG diagrams, full original editorial copy, accessible lightbox and mobile navigation, robust local drafts/submissions/inbox, downloadable summaries, 13 designed 1200×630 covers including fallback, accurate exported metadata. Typecheck, lint, focused tests, production export, direct-route/static checks and actual desktop/mobile browser QA. Document limitations honestly.
